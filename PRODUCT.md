## Platform
Native macOS 14+ / Apple Silicon.
## Stack
Swift 6, SwiftUI/AppKit, official DuckDB Swift pinned at 1.1.3, Swift Charts. Build from SwiftPM.
## Product Purpose
Local Data Studio and Statistical Lab sharing versioned datasets, provenance, quality findings and reproducible analyses. The supplied ORDEN_CODEX_VAPULA_v0.1.md is the technical brief; the destination is confirmed by the user as /Users/sebastian/Desktop/CODEX/VAPULA.
## Users
Inferred audience: researchers and analysts working with local tabular data. No specific institutional audience is assumed.
## Capabilities and Constraints
No runtime network, accounts, telemetry, online AI, updates, SQL console or modification of imported sources. Data must remain portable inside .vapula packages. Numerical results require independent references. Unsupported computations fail visibly.
## Brand Commitments
VAPULA / Data & Statistical Laboratory / Creado por Sebastián Pastén F. Monochrome geometric reinterpretation of the historical Vapula seal, related to METATRON without copying its cube. Native sidebar, system typography, light/dark, keyboard and VoiceOver.

Synthetic fixtures and independently generated SciPy development references. Benchmarks and validation are recorded only after execution.
